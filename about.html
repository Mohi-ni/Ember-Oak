Where It Started

Ember & Oak opened in 2015 with one idea: cook everything the way it used to be cooked, over real fire, with ingredients from people we actually know. What began as a single wood-fired grill in a small kitchen has grown into a full café and bistro — but the fire is still where every dish starts.
